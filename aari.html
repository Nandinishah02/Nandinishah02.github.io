Aari Embroidery

Gulbadan emerged from my experience of working closely with Aari artisans in the state of Gujarat and observing the patience, rhythm, and care embedded within their process. Inspired by blooming flowers and softness within nature, the collection combines handcrafted embroidery with fluid silhouettes, reinterpreting traditional techniques through a contemporary lens while celebrating the human connection behind the craft.
