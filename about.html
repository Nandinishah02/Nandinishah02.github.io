--- title: About me ---

Hey, I’m Nandini, a textile and fashion designer based between India and New York City.

Working primarily with embroidery, my practice explores the labour, time, and care embedded within handmade processes, bringing attention to the unseen and often overlooked aspects of craft and garment-making.

You can follow me on Instagram at @s.nandiniii, or email me at shahnandini2402@gmail.com.
